About This Tool

An open planning tool for flood-exposed cities in eastern Ethiopia. Combines machine-learning flood susceptibility under current and projected climate with station-based extreme-rainfall statistics.

Methods

Susceptibility: Sentinel-1 SAR & Machine Learning (SSP2-4.5 & SSP5-8.5).
Rainfall: GEV distribution fitted by L-moments, cross-validated against POT and regional frequency analysis (72 station-years).
