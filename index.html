About this tool

An open planning tool for the flood-exposed cities of eastern Ethiopia. It combines machine-learning flood susceptibility mapping under current and projected climate with station-based extreme-rainfall statistics, so planners can see both where flooding is likely and how intense the driving rainfall is. It covers … woredas and districts.

Methods

Susceptibility: Sentinel-1 SAR and machine learning, projected under SSP2-4.5 and SSP5-8.5.

Rainfall: Generalized Extreme Value distribution fitted by L-moments, cross-validated against peaks-over-threshold, with regional frequency analysis over a statistically homogeneous region.

Data sources

Rainfall data: National Meteorology Institute of Ethiopia.
Administrative boundaries: FEWS NET.
